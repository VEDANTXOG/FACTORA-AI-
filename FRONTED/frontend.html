HOW IT WORKS

  • ◇ Checks Sources: Checks your given article from reputed sources, trusted news outlets all around the world.
  • ◇ Provide Verdict: Gives you a clear verdict like real, likely, fake or suspicious.
  • ◇ Learn While Checking: Offers insight into why the article may be misleading and highlights primary signals.
  • WHAT ARE : ◇ Manipulation Score

    What it is: Measures how much article uses misleading language.

    • Analyses emotional words, urgency, exaggeration, fake authority, etc.

    Score Range: 0 - 1.0

    • 0.0 - 0.3: Minimal manipulation (like straight forward reporting)

    • 0.4 - 0.6: Some manipulation detected (Stay cautious)

    • 0.7 - 1.0: High manipulation (Strongly misleading or fake-sounding contents)

  • ◇ Verification Score : What It is :

    1]Measures how much Factora AI can trust the news based on source reliability and contextual evidence

    2]Checks for respectable sources , authority phrases and factual reporting cues

    Score range :

    0.0 - 0.3 : Low Confidence (Article may be false or unverified)

    0.4 - 0.6 : Medium Confidence (verify it correctly)

    0.7 - 1.0 : High confidence (Sources and context strongly suggest it is real)

  • Primary Signal : What It is :

    Shows the main type of manupulation or attention catching techniques detected in the article
